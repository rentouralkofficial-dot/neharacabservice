PRICING NOTE
------------
The calculator handles *only* the published 5km/10km starting tiers and extra-km rate above 10km.
The original rate sheet contains additional rules for up/down trips, drops between 100-130km,
waiting, long distances, labour, and other charges. These are NOT automatically combined into a
payable total until the company confirms the exact calculation rules and applicability.
NEHARA confirms final total and availability directly.
Car/van prices, 40ft transport price not yet supplied -> quote on request.
